想い出

A song that sounds like the last hour of a summer day:
warm asphalt, salt in the air, the radio low.

Bright strings and a lazy groove carry a quiet goodbye,
pure 80s Tokyo city pop, still glowing decades on.

This little page is a love letter to it. 🌅

レコード

The record

サンセット・ロード came out in 1987 as a 7-inch single on Victor, the second and final single from Reiko Takahashi (高橋玲子). She was one of the many young idol singers of the decade and left behind only a handful of recordings, which makes this one feel like a postcard from a career that barely had time to begin.

作家たち

The writers

The song came from a team who, as one Japanese collector put it, could hardly have made anything but city pop.

作曲 · music

濱田金吾Kingo Hamada

A key name in Japanese AOR and city pop. He came up through the folk-rock group Craft (クラフト), went solo with Manhattan in the Rain (1980, partly recorded in New York), and wrote songs for singers such as Mariya Takeuchi, Hiromi Ohta, Hiromi Iwasaki, Mariko Takahashi and Hideki Saijo.

編曲 · arrangement

奥慶一Keiichi Oku

A keyboardist, composer and arranger born in Shiga in 1955. He debuted in 1979 as a member of the band Spectrum, released his solo album Misty Morning in 1981, and went on to arrange kayōkyoku hits and score anime. He is behind the shimmering arrangement you hear here.

作詞 · lyrics

佐藤純子Junko Sato

Wrote the words, a bittersweet summer goodbye by the sea.

再発見

Found again

City pop (シティポップ) was the glossy, urban pop of 1970s–80s Japan. It drew on AOR, funk, soft rock and boogie, and was made for car stereos and city nights. In the 2010s, YouTube recommendations and streaming carried it to listeners all over the world who had never heard of most of these artists.

Sunset Road was one of the songs swept up in that wave. Original copies of the single are now sought-after records, and the song has been streamed millions of times by listeners overseas, enough for one Japanese writer to call Takahashi a "reverse-imported" city pop artist.
